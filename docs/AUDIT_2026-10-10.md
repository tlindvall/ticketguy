## 1. Bottom line

1. **What works today (main, real mail).** A US customer who emails a named event, a marketplace link or a readable screenshot gets a threaded, de-duplicated reply that names the event from Ticketmaster Discovery, reads the screenshot through gpt-6.1-sol, quotes SeatData resale floors (listed, before fees) and gives a verdict with one next step. Real mail has been answered since 2026-09-24 (docs/FEATURE_STATUS.md:20; DECISION_LOG #47 is the first entry describing live threads, #74 the latest). How fast replies arrive has no recorded measurement.
2. **What this branch fixes (fixture-tested, not live).** Of the 45 audit gaps, 28 are fixed, 1 is fixed in part (18), 1 was not reproduced (35) and 15 stay open; the seven items that were in flight during the audit are finished too. In short:
   - A thread keeps its facts across side questions, closed requests, settled games and a change of act.
   - A clarification asks one question.
   - The newest link or screenshot is the one judged, two sent together are compared, and the reply says whether a listing lookup actually ran.
   - The brief keeps a budget's fee basis, a decision deadline and a ranking goal ("best view", "cheapest is fine"), and picks follow the goal.
   - An over-budget party on a falling market can be told to hold off without a watch.
   - A customer who writes while a draft waits for review gets a note, and an expired draft is researched again instead of sent.
   - Every automated reply now says we never buy, hold or resell tickets.
   - /api/health reports outbox lag.
   
   The brief's seven requests and six follow-ups run end to end through dispatch in tests/acceptance/brief-journeys-1010.test.ts: 20 of 20 pass in both policy modes.
3. **What still does not work.**
   - Nothing is purchasable or checkout-verified: no seller adapter exists, and all 135 rows in research/ticket-guy-us-source-registry.json are not_integrated.
   - Watches, on-sale alerts and follow-ups are switched off.
   - StubHub pick links rest on an unproven listing-id equivalence (gap 7).
   - The screenshot reader has no recorded evaluation (gap 27).
   - The open-web event finder is unreachable (gap 23).
   - How often buy-or-wait answers rather than abstains in production is unknown. The audit's "almost always abstains" has no source: no document summarises the market.trend_assessed audits (#73), and the thresholds (MARKET_MIN_POINTS = 4, src/lib/market/series.ts:132; stale and span checks at 315-317) say only when it may answer.
4. **The single biggest risk, unchanged by this branch: the launch cliff (gap 1).**
   - Every live recommendation is system-approved under the testing exemption: AUTO_APPROVE_WHILE_TESTING with a non-empty allowlist (pipeline.ts:189-190).
   - Suppose EMAIL_TEST_RECIPIENT_ALLOWLIST is emptied without AUTO_SEND_RECOMMENDATIONS=true set in the dashboard and the SeatData licence row approved for advice and customer_display. Then every brief either waits in a review queue nobody staffs, or goes out with every price, trend and cheaper-equivalent line removed (src/lib/market/tracker.ts:38-41).
   - Neither AUTO_SEND_RECOMMENDATIONS nor AUTO_APPROVE_WHILE_TESTING is declared in render.yaml.
   
   Close behind: the test_mode switch still captures real customers' replies and records them as sent (gap 2). Which scheduler drains the outbox is still unrecorded. The branch adds outbox lag to /api/health, but no external alert on it.
5. **Deployed state is unknown.**
   - Main now includes the Oct 3 take-charge rule and product choice, and the Oct 9 ranked games, prominence and subject verdicts. All of these are newer than every build with a recorded live check (dadd21f and b31b3fc, both Oct 2), and no DECISION_LOG or docs/qa entry after #73 records a post-deploy check.
   - Everything on this branch needs a merge, a deploy and a live check (owner decision 12) before any "fixed" in section 5 counts as live.
