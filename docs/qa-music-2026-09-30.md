## Remaining limitations

This is a deterministic reader of supplied text, with conservative unknowns for unsupported wording; it is not an unrestricted natural-language policy verifier. Event-specific policy freshness, original artist appearances, current inventory, guaranteed seats and all-in checkout totals still require reliable source evidence. No licensed inventory or trend integration was added. Overnight comparisons use the supplied venue-local clock and calendar dates; missing chronology is not guessed.

No merge, deployment, Render change, test-mode change, cap change or real email delivery is part of this work. Browser/email-client visual QA and fresh live QA remain with the originating research chat.
